Storage

Store the product in a dry, clean place with proper ventilation. Storage temperature and humidity:

  • 1 month: -20 °C to 45 °C (0-60 % RH)
  • 3 months: 0 °C to 45 °C (0-60 % RH)
  • 12 months: 0 °C to 25 °C (0-60 % RH)

If this product is stored for a long period of time (3 months - 6 months) with the power depleted, it may become unchargeable. To prevent this and maintain battery health, it is recommended to check and recharge the product every three months. See Troubleshooting.

Perform a full charge and discharge cycle at least once every 6 to 12 months.
