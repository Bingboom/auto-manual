# SAFETY PRECAUTIONS WHEN USING

## Solar Panel Safety Tips

<ul><li><p>Do not flex or bend the folding solar panels.</p></li><li><p>Do not place folding solar panels in shading places, while tree shades, buildings, and any other obstructions should be minimized.</p></li><li><p>Do not soak the solar panels in water or other liquid.</p></li><li><p>Do not clean the solar panel with water, please gently wipe it with a damp cloth.</p></li><li><p>Do not use or store solar panels near open flames or flammable materials.</p></li><li><p>Do not scratch the surface of solar panels with sharp objects.</p></li><li><p>Prevent corrosive substances to damage solar panels.</p></li><li><p>Do not step on or place heavy objects on the solar panels.</p></li><li><p>Do not disassemble the solar panels.</p></li><li><p>The output circuit of the solar panels must be correctly connected to the equipment. Please do not short-circuit the positive and negative poles. Ensure that there is no gap between the connector and the insulated connector. If there is a gap, sparks or electrical shocks will occur.</p></li><li><p>Check the connection status of each component, including cables, wires and plugs.</p></li></ul>
