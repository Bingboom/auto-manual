SAFETY PRECAUTIONS WHEN USING

Make sure Jackery Explorer 2000 Plus and Jackery Battery Pack 2000 Plus are not in operation
when connecting or disconnecting them.

SOLAR CHARGING

This product supports solar charging. You can refer to the user manual of Jackery Explorer 2000
Plus to know how to connect the solar panel.

CHARGING IN THE CAR

This product can be charged with a 12V car charger. The car charger cable of Jackery Explorer
2000 Plus should be used for connection when charging. Please refer to the Jackery Explorer
2000 Plus user manual for precautions.
