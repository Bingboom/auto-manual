# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JE-2000D US English manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout and encoding decisions only.

1. Cover (physical 1), the paper CONTENTS page (physical 2) and the back cover (physical 14: JACKERY INC. address, contact line, QR) are not on the Web page; the running US tabs and printed page numbers are dropped.
2. The p01 section band (US English / Model: JE-2000D) is a shared hb-text-panel before the intro paragraphs.
3. The TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS heading is the HB-TABLE-SPEC section title (the shared spec carrier draws it as the chapter h2). The DC Input value's two print lines are one wrapped value.
4. CERTIFICATIONS marks are three images in one paragraph (FCC and WEEE shared catalog files, BC the JE-1000D US native file); the p08 FCC mark is its own native paths.
5. PACKAGE LIST ①–③ become the numbered cards of HB-SPECIAL-INBOX; the shared component draws the numbers.
6. The ①–⑲ LCD legend printed in three columns across p02/p03 is one HB-TABLE-REFERENCE/lcd-descriptions list in number order.
7. Each LCD description block (icon, bold title, text) becomes a row of HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON (unnumbered), in printed order, in two tables (p03 and p04 groups) with the Energy Saving Mode table and its on/off instructions between them as printed.
8. The Energy Saving Mode table keeps its printed Defaults cell spanning the three port rows (declared plain table).
9. Bold run-in titles printed in the small bold style (UPS:, To turn off/on the Energy Saving Mode, LCD Screen On/Off, LCD is off automatically, Emergency Charging Mode, SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS) stay bold paragraphs; section headings printed in the larger style are h3.
10. The WARNING: / DANGER: run-in notices (p08) and the Remarks: notes (p10, p11) become HB-CALLOUT-STRIP warning / danger / note. The shared strip shows the signal word without its printed trailing colon (callout label normalisation, as in the JE-1000D editions); the words are unchanged. The ※ not-waterproof line after DANGER stays a plain paragraph.
11. Bullet glyphs (·, grey dots) are list markers of shared lists. The a)–m) safety items keep their printed letters as text in one paragraph each.
12. The Note: 1./2. charging notes, *-prefixed notes and Tip: stay plain paragraphs as printed (no frame in the print). The bold weight of the leading asterisks is not reproduced.
13. The 2.1 「+」 add-device glyph is native text in HB-SPECIAL-APP/inline-control.
14. Figure captions and leader labels printed inside panels stay in the panel art and are carried as alt text and IR metadata, not repeated as visible text. The Emergency Charging note printed beside the AC charging drawing is a paragraph before the figure.
15. Warranty sections (including Interpretation Rights) are h3 headings with paragraphs (the print draws no cards). Download the Jackery Mobile App is an h3 followed by the complete badge pair.
16. On phones, the overview and LCD map pan sideways inside the figure; the LCD description and Energy Saving Mode tables scroll inside the shared table wrapper.
17. Encoding of the outlined print: U+2393 ⎓ for the DC symbol, U+00D7 × only where the raised multiplication sign is printed, U+00B0 for every degree sign, U+FF0C / U+FF1A for the printed full-width comma (DANGER) and colon (4.3), curly or straight quotes as printed. Multiple spaces visible in the print are kept in the transcription; HTML displays them as one.
