# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JHP-2000A-RC US V2 manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout decisions only.

1. Cover (p1), paper table of contents (p3) and back cover (p24) are not on the Web page; printed page numbers and the US language badge beside IMPORTANT are dropped.
2. Chapter titles are shared h2 strips; subsection bullets (FRONT VIEW, POWER ON/OFF …) are shared h3 headings.
3. The FCC panel is HB-SPECIAL-FCC in its own section; the shared theme hides that section's navigation-only "FCC" title (the print has no title). The ISED/RF panel beside it is the shared hb-text-panel.
4. The WARNING / DANGER boxes and every CAUTION / NOTE / WARNING side-label box become HB-CALLOUT-STRIP with the printed label; bullets inside them are list items. The middle-dot (·) list markers in the UPS warning are list structure.
5. The signal table (WARNING, CAUTION, NOTE, TIP) is HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-SIGNAL; the printed badge icons are drawn by the shared badge. The pictogram tables are HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-ICON in the printed 6 + 5 panel order.
6. The 26 LCD descriptions are one numbered HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON in printed order across the three printed pages; ㉓ prints two icons (high/low temperature) and keeps the number on both rows.
7. In operation panels, the callout bubble, prerequisite pills, explanatory text boxes, LED step texts, UPS explanation and AC charging caption are native text (hb-text-panel or ordered list) placed after or before the art; the panel art keeps its leader labels (On / Off / Press once …), which are listed as embedded labels in the IR.
8. LCD SCREEN is HB-TABLE-LCD-MODE with its artwork; KEY COMBINATION is HB-TABLE-KEY-COMBINATIONS with the shared button carrier; AC AND DC OUTPUT RESUME FUNCTION is HB-TABLE-AUTO-RESUME. These shared tables keep a fixed minimum width and scroll inside the component when the column is narrower.
9. "SOLD SEPARATELY" pills use the shared hb-sold-separately label (beside the title in h3.hb-heading-label-pair for solar and car charging; alone under the battery-pack title).
10. "Fully charge the product before its first use." (a dark pill in print) is a bold paragraph; "Emergency Charging Mode" (a pill) is an h4.
11. Specification groups are HB-TABLE-SPEC tables; multi-value cells keep the printed line order.
12. Troubleshooting is HB-TABLE-TROUBLESHOOTING; numbered steps keep their printed numbers and line breaks.
13. Warranty: lead box HB-WARRANTY-LEAD, titled cards are a heading (the printed pill) plus HB-WARRANTY-SECTION, the 3/2 YEARS cards HB-WARRANTY-YEARS.
14. App: download badges and QR are HB-SPECIAL-APP/download; the 2.1 「+」 glyph is native text in HB-SPECIAL-APP/inline-control; screenshots and the control-panel diagram are complete panels.
15. On phones, the overview views, LCD map and control-panel diagram pan sideways inside the figure.
