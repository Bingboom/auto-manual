# JS-100F source discovery and component plan

Material 38-0001-000644 is the current English JS-100F / SolarSaga 100 single-language attachment: seven image-only pages, cover badge V2. The source bytes and live attachment coordinates are in intake-source.json. All pages were viewed, and the OCR was corrected line by line against the page pixels.

Two pages are not body content and are declared omissions:

- page 2 is the printed contents locator;
- the cover drawing and wordmark are decorative.

The model, the V2 badge and the contact lines are retained.

The parameters page prints a three-column STC/BNPI table with values that span both conditions. It stays one declared plain table, as the JS-100I JP spec-table edition does, and the Multifunctional Adapter rows use HB-TABLE-SPEC. Other mappings:

- The printed "Tip:" uses HB-CALLOUT-STRIP.
- The four diagrams use HB-SPECIAL-REFERENCE-FIGURE.
- The prose warranty, contact and customer-service blocks use HB-WARRANTY-SECTION. No years graphic is synthesized.
- The FCC mark is the shared SVG.
- The NOM mark and QR are source crops.

No LCD, App, inbox or symbol table is printed, and none is added.

The booklet carries Mexico content (NOM, hello.mx, JACKERY MEXICO S.A. DE C.V.) and the print spec HTO587H-MX-JAK, against the Feishu finished-goods spec HTO587H-US-JAK. This is raised as an operator decision; the copy stays as printed.
