## Sun Angle Indicator

<p>The carry handle has a sun angle indicator. When sunlight hits its surface, a shadow will appear at its bottom.</p>

<p>If the shadow falls on the white inner circle at its bottom, it means that the solar panel is facing directly towards sun and you can get optimal power generation; if not, it is suggested to adjust its angle until it does.</p>
