Front View Rear View

The Jackery SolarSaga 100 Light supports both adhesive mounting
and suspension mounting.

@ Adhesive Mounting

Note: Adhesive is not included. Please purchase a suitable adhesive separately.

* Do not attach this product to walls using adhesive methods.

* This type of adhesive installation is suitable only for low and smooth
surfaces, such as the exterior surfaces of vehicles like cars or yachts.
Strictly avoid sticking or mounting this product onto the exterior walls
of high-rise buildings, as it may create serious safety risks.
