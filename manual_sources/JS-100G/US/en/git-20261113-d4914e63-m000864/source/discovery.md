# JS-100G source discovery and component plan

Material 38-0001-000864 is the current English JS-100G / SolarSaga 100 Light single-language attachment: seventeen image-only pages, with no printed manual version. The source bytes and live attachment coordinates are in intake-source.json. All pages were viewed, and the OCR was corrected line by line against the page pixels.

Two pages are not body content and are declared omissions:

- page 2 is the printed table of contents;
- the cover drawing and wordmark are decorative.

The model and the cover contact box are retained.

Mappings to shared components:

- WARNINGS becomes a native list.
- FCC uses the shared FCC SVG with native text.
- WHAT'S IN THE BOX uses HB-SPECIAL-INBOX with four cards.
- INSTALLATION keeps the printed order: front/rear view, adhesive mounting (one Note, two icon-only warning boxes, six step panels), suspension mounting (one Note, a three-card Wire Kit inbox, rope clamp and tensioner figures, nine step panels, one icon-only warning box) and the series/parallel connection notes with two figures.
- TECHNICAL PARAMETERS is the STC*/BNPI* three-column table, a declared plain table, with footnotes.
- WARRANTY uses HB-WARRANTY-YEARS (5 YEARS) and HB-WARRANTY-SECTION.
- Contacts sit on the back cover.

The icon-only warning boxes carry no signal word, so the shared callout uses the printed ⚠ glyph as its label. No WARNING word is invented.

No existing art matches this product. All 29 illustrations and the QR are new source-bound extractions; only the FCC mark is shared.
