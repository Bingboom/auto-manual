# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JE-3000B US en manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout decisions only.

1. Cover (p1), paper TABLE OF CONTENTS (p3) and back cover (p19: JACKERY INC. address, contact, QR) are not on the Web page; printed page numbers are dropped. The preface (p2) opens the page before the first chapter; its 「US」 badge is print-only chrome.
2. Chapter titles are shared h2 strips; the risk band, GROUNDING / USER MAINTENANCE / SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS / MOVING AND STORAGE pills and the round-bullet sub-titles are h3; App 4.1–4.3 are h4.
3. The WARNING box, the 「Risk of Electric Shock」 box beside it and the bullets printed under them are one HB-CALLOUT-STRIP/warning (bold leads, then the list). The grounding and battery-pack WARNING boxes and the DANGER box are HB-CALLOUT-STRIP in their variant; the shared label slot carries 「WARNING」 / 「DANGER」 without the printed colon. Important, Note and Remarks boxes are HB-CALLOUT-STRIP/note with the printed label.
4. The FCC statement is HB-SPECIAL-FCC (two columns as printed) inside a generic docutils container so MOVING AND STORAGE INSTRUCTIONS keeps its heading; the printed 「--」 item markers are the shared list markers.
5. LCD DISPLAY: the 19 numbered rows are one HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON. Rows ④ (Battery Saving / Self-powered) and ⑯ (High / Low temperature) print one number and one glyph for two sub-rows; on the Web each sub-row repeats the number and carries the small glyph printed beside its name. The small 「can be enabled / disabled in the Jackery APP」 / 「can be set in the Jackery APP」 line stays under the name.
6. Operation panels are complete source panels. Grey pills and bubbles printed inside them (the three 「Premise:」 pills, the standby-time and reset-button bubbles, the UPS connection bubble, the car-cable note) and the AC wall charging instruction are removed from the art by deleting their vector objects (no overpainting) and set as native text — in the shared grey `hb-text-panel` group for the boxed ones, as a paragraph for the unboxed instruction. A premise pill printed at the top of a panel comes before the figure; a bubble printed inside the lower part comes after it.
7. LCD SCREEN is HB-TABLE-LCD-MODE (two states × three actions) with its artwork; KEY COMBINATIONS is HB-TABLE-KEY-COMBINATIONS with this PDF's native button paths, the shared clock duration mark and the shared emphasis on button names.
8. TROUBLESHOOTING keeps its three printed columns as a declared plain table (`shared_component_exceptions`); codes and names are bold, the regular-weight slashes inside the F8/F9 names are bold with them, and each printed description line is its own paragraph.
9. Numbered notes (1.–4.) keep their numbers in the paragraph text; 「·」 / 「•」 lists are shared lists.
10. Warranty: the bold lead and the *note are HB-WARRANTY-LEAD; each titled card is the printed title pill (h3) plus HB-WARRANTY-SECTION; 3 YEARS / 2 YEARS is HB-WARRANTY-YEARS. The Exclusions items are printed without bullets and stay paragraphs.
11. App: the download pair is HB-SPECIAL-APP/download, the 2.1 「+」 glyph is native text in HB-SPECIAL-APP/inline-control; the two 「·」 lines printed under the second Remarks box are a list after it.
12. SPECIFICATIONS: the printed band 「Jackery Explorer 3000 / Model: JE-3000B」 above the heading is a bold shared grey panel under the SPECIFICATIONS h2. The four groups are HB-TABLE-SPEC/vertical tables titled by the printed group titles; the three DC Input lines stay in one cell and the second Discharging Temperature line is its own row.
13. Straight and curly quotes stay as printed: the Web build disables Sphinx smart quotes. Printed double spaces are kept in the source and collapse in HTML.
14. On phones, overview panels and the LCD map pan sideways inside the figure; the LCD, key-combination and troubleshooting tables scroll inside their shared wrappers (also on desktop for the 44rem key-combination table).
