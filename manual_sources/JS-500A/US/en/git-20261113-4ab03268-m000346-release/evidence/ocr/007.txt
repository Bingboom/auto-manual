The panel can be fastened with straps, screws or ropes in a location that receives
ample sunlight, such as a balcony or rooftop.

Note:

1. The panel is not intended for permanent installation.

2. Before installing a rooftop solar system, check your local building codes and obtain
all required permits from the appropriate city or county authority.
