## BASIC OPERATIONS

### Power On/Off

<p><strong>AC Output On/Off:</strong> Press the AC Button, the AC Output operation indicator lights up. Then you can connect the equipment that needs AC charging. Press the AC Button again to turn off the AC Output.</p>

<p><strong>USB Output On/Off:</strong> Press the USB Button, the USB Output operation indicator lights up. Then USB-A and USB-C Output Ports can be connected to external load devices. Press the USB Button again to turn off the USB Output.</p>

<p><strong>Car Port On/Off:</strong> Press the Car Port Button, the Car Port operation indicator lights up. Then the Car Port can be connected to the external load devices. Press the Car Port Button again to turn off the output.</p>

<figure class="hb-reference-figure" data-component-id="HB-SPECIAL-REFERENCE-FIGURE" data-reference-id="operations" data-source-fragment-sha256="c87402d35b12f7fe7b077a399dc5b77652905cb861da3aee75b022add86d847e" data-step-captions="embedded"><div class="hb-reference-semantic" data-reference-id="operations.semantic"><img alt="Car Port Button / AC Button / USB Button" class="hb-reference-art hb-composite-art" src="assets/operations.png"/></div></figure>

### LCD Screen On/Off

<p>To Turn On, press the DISPLAY Button, any Output Power Button, or LED Light Button. Or, when there’s a charging input, the screen display will light up automatically.</p>

<p>To Turn Off, press the DISPLAY Button again and the screen display will turn off. Or, if there is no operation in 30 seconds, the product will enter a sleep state and the screen display will automatically turn off.</p>

<p><strong>To get the screen always-on (under charging or discharging state), follow these steps:</strong> After the display screen lights up, double-click the DISPLAY Button and the mode of Always-On Display will be on.</p>

<p>To turn off Always-On Display, follow these steps: Press the DISPLAY Button and the screen will be turned off in 30 seconds.</p>

<table class="manual-callout-table manual-callout-table"><tbody><tr><td class="manual-callout-label">Tip</td><td class="manual-callout-body"><p>During Always-On mode, the screen automatically turns off after 2 hours without charging or discharging.</p></td></tr></tbody></table>

### LED Light On/Off

<p><strong>There are three modes of LED Light:</strong> Low Light mode, Bright Light mode, and SOS mode.</p>

<p><strong>The operations are as follows:</strong> Press the LED Light Button, the LED will be turned on. The light will always turn on in Low Light mode and the output power on the screen is 1W. Press again to enter the Bright Light mode and the output power on the screen is 3W. Press the third time to enter the SOS mode and the output power on the screen is 3W. Then, press again to turn off the LED Light. In any of these modes, the light can be turned off by a long press.</p>

<p><strong>Green energy first:</strong> We advocate to use the green energy first. This product supports two modes of charging at the same time: solar charging and AC wall charging.</p>

<p>When AC wall charging and solar charging are turned on at the same time, the product will give priority to solar charging and both methods will be used to charge the battery at the maximum permissible power.</p>

## FAQ

<p><strong>Q: Can the product be brought on board a plane?</strong></p>

<p>A: No, because this product is a lithium battery. According to the International Standards &amp; Regulations for the Transportation of Hazardous Materials, no lithium batteries with more than 100 watt hours may be allowed in carry-on bags.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Why is there sound when using?</strong></p>

<p>A: The product adopts an air-cooled system, and the built-in fan helps the product to cool better. Slight noise when using is normal.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Can I charge the Jackery Explorer 2000 Pro while a device is plugged in?</strong></p>

<p>A: Yes, you can. When charged with AC input, it supports a maximum power output of 1800W. When charged with AC input + DC input ( i.e. solar charging or car charging) simultaneously - it supports a maximum power output of 1800W. When charged with DC input (i.e. solar charging or car charging), it supports a maximum power output of 2200W.</p>

<p><strong>Q: How to keep this product when it is not used for a long time?</strong></p>

<p>A: If the battery is to be unused for a long time (more than 3 months), it should be maintained at 60-80% of the rated capacity (it's recommended to check and charge every 3 months). Store it in a dry and cool place with temperature range between 0°C~40°C, and humidity 0%~60%. Avoid contact with corrosive substances, and far from fire and heat sources.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Can it be used to jump start a car?</strong></p>

<p>A: We are very sorry, but it cannot be used to jump start a vehicle. However, it can be used as power supply for automobiles. You can recharge the car via a Jackery power supply cord, which is available for purchase separately.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Why is the output to the low-power device automatically turned off after several hours?</strong></p>

<p>A: In case of forgetting to turn off the output (causing the battery to drain), the Low Power Mode will be turned off by default. That is, when the port is connected to a small load device(AC Output≤25W, USB Output≤2W, Car Port≤2W) for 12 hours, the device will automatically be turned off. In case you want to resume recharging, press the car port button/USB button/AC button to restart the device. If you want to turn on the Low Power Mode, long press the AC Button and DISPLAY until the Low Power Mode icon <img alt="Low Power Mode" src="assets/icons/faq-low-power-mode.png"/> appears on the screen. If you need to turn off the Low Power Mode, repeat the above operation until <img alt="Low Power Mode" src="assets/icons/faq-low-power-mode.png"/> .</p>

<p><strong>Q: Why does the USB Output operation indicator light up, the load device has no charging input or can't work normally?</strong></p>

<p>A: Please check whether the load device is broken firstly. If the device is normal, press the USB Button to restart the output.</p>
