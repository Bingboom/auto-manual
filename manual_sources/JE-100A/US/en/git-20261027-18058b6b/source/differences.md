# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JE-100A US English manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout decisions only.

1. The cover (p1), the contact-page QR code (p10), printed page numbers and the 「US」 corner tabs are not on the Web page.
2. The dark band 「US English | Model: JE-100A」 is the first chapter h2; its two printed parts are joined with a space. The other chapter titles are shared h2 headings, sub-headings h3 and the bold solar-guide titles h4.
3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS is HB-TABLE-SPEC under the band chapter (its title is the shared spec section title); labels keep their printed colons.
4. The CERTIFICATIONS mark cluster is one complete figure; its conformity text stays in the art and in the alt text.
5. PACKAGE LIST cards are HB-SPECIAL-INBOX; the shared cards draw 1–3 ordinals the print does not show.
6. The LCD icon blocks (icon, bold title, text) are two unnumbered HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON tables in printed order, with the two 「To turn on/off the Energy Saving Mode」 items between them as headings and paragraphs.
7. The FCC statement is HB-SPECIAL-FCC with the shared FC mark: the NOTE paragraph on the left, the measures and MODIFICATION on the right. The printed 「--」 measure lines are kept verbatim as list items, so the shared list adds its bullet before each 「--」.
8. Warranty sub-headings are h3 headings followed by HB-WARRANTY-SECTION cards; the contact blocks of p10 close the WARRANTY chapter in shared text panels.
9. Figure captions and leader labels printed inside panels stay in the panel art and are carried as alt text and IR metadata, not repeated as visible text.
10. On phones the product appearance and LCD map pan sideways inside the figure; LCD tables scroll inside the shared component.
