# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JE-5000A US manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout decisions only.

1. Cover (p1), paper TABLE OF CONTENTS (p3) and back cover (p27: JACKERY INC. address and contact) are not on the Web page; printed page numbers are dropped. The IMPORTANT preface (p2) is the first Web chapter; its printed 「US」 language tab is print-only chrome.
2. Chapter titles are shared h2 strips, following the print TOC. The dark product bands of the AC ESS guide (Jackery Explorer 5000 Plus, Jackery Battery Pack 5000 Plus, Jackery Smart Transfer Switch) print in the same strip style and are h2 as well, each followed by its bold 「Model: …」 line. Round-bullet sub-titles (FRONT VIEW, POWER ON/OFF, BACKUP UPS, CHARGING WITH …, KEY COMBINATIONS, PACKAGE LIST, 1.–4. App steps) and the risk band, OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS, USER MAINTENANCE INSTRUCTIONS and MEANING OF SYMBOLS pills are h3; SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS, the • Connect to … / High-PV + Low-PV Input leads and App 4.1–4.3 are h4.
3. The shared specification table always titles its group as an h2 (`h2.hb-spec-section`), so GENERAL INFO, INPUT PORTS … appear as h2 in the page outline, also inside the AC ESS guide.
4. The WARNING box and the bullets printed under it are one HB-CALLOUT-STRIP/warning: the bold lead is the first body line, the bullets its list. DANGER, Note, Caution and Remarks boxes are HB-CALLOUT-STRIP with the printed label (Remarks uses the note variant). Numbered notes keep their printed numbers as text, one paragraph per number.
5. The FCC statement printed below the symbol tables is HB-SPECIAL-FCC inside a generic docutils container, so MEANING OF SYMBOLS keeps its visible heading; the NOTE paragraph that runs from the left into the right column is one paragraph.
6. The WARNING/CAUTION signal table is HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-SIGNAL with the printed Symbol/Meaning headers; the badges are drawn by the shared component. The 8-symbol table is HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-ICON with its two printed panels (4 + 4 rows).
7. WHAT'S IN THE BOX is HB-SPECIAL-INBOX with the five numbered cards and the Note.
8. Product overview: FRONT VIEW (with the bottom AC outlet view), LEFT SIDE VIEW and RIGHT SIDE VIEW are complete panels with their leader labels in the art and in alt text / IR metadata. The two side views share a strip of the print: the left view is cut just above the retractable-handle inset of the right view (losing the bottom 2 pt of its caster), the right view starts just below the left view's wheels (losing the top 2.6 pt of the inset circle) and omits the printed RIGHT SIDE VIEW heading (it is the native h3).
9. LCD: the 23 numbers are one numbered HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON across printed pages 05–06 with shared number cells. Rows ⑫, ⑬ and ⑳ print a combined icon in the icon column plus a sub-icon beside each sub-row name; the Web rows show each sub-row's own icon and the combined icon is not repeated. The small App/STS hints printed under row names (「can be enabled / disabled in the Jackery APP」, 「Ensure the STS is successfully connected before proceeding.」) are small native text in the name cell. Printed bold On:/Blink:/Off:/On/Off: stay bold.
10. Operation panels are complete source panels. Grey text boxes and pills printed inside them (the standby-time bubble, the three 「Prerequisite: …」 pills), the DC vehicle sentence, the UPS and STS bubbles, the AC wall and STS charging sentences, the MC4 wrench sentence, the Lock/Unlock titles and sentences, the car-cable note and the AC ESS caption are removed from the art by deleting their text and vector objects (no overpainting) and set as native text in the shared grey `hb-text-panel` or as paragraphs directly before or after the panel. On/Off/Press once labels, 3s, the clearance labels, STEP 1/2, *STS App, DC8020, SolarSaga, Vehicle, App captions and package labels stay in the art and in alt text / IR metadata.
11. LCD SCREEN is HB-TABLE-LCD-MODE (two states × three actions) with its artwork; KEY COMBINATIONS is HB-TABLE-KEY-COMBINATIONS with the printed button art (native POWER/USB/AC, JHP-5000C DC), the shared clock duration mark and the shared emphasis on button names.
12. TROUBLESHOOTING prints three columns (Error Code / Name / Description); HB-TABLE-TROUBLESHOOTING has two, so it is a declared plain table on the shared manual-table classes (`shared_component_exceptions`) with bold codes and names and the printed line breaks of F6 and F7.
13. SPECIFICATIONS: each printed group is an HB-TABLE-SPEC/vertical table; a label printed once beside two value rows (Bypass Mode AC Input, Low-PV, Discharging Temperature) spans both rows; the empty 「DC Input」 value cell stays empty. The Smart Transfer Switch GENERAL INFO continues from printed page 22 to 23 and is one table. The Explorer 5000 Plus tables of printed page 21 repeat those of printed page 19 and both are kept.
14. The INGESTION HAZARD notices show the printed glyph inline (a staged native variant until the shared catalog carries it) before the bold label.
15. Warranty: the bold lead and the *Warranty period note are HB-WARRANTY-LEAD; each titled card is the printed title pill (h3) plus HB-WARRANTY-SECTION; the Exclusions items print as paragraphs and stay paragraphs; 5 YEARS / 2 YEARS is HB-WARRANTY-YEARS.
16. App: the download pair is HB-SPECIAL-APP/download, the 2.1 「+」 glyph is native text in HB-SPECIAL-APP/inline-control; phone screenshots and the button map are complete panels; 「The above screenshots are for reference only.」 is native text below them.
17. The System Model HB5000A-TS02A tag of the AC ESS title strip is a shared hb-text-panel below the chapter heading. The PACKAGE LIST is three complete panels (the dashed group frames and the 「Sold separately」 badges stay in the art).
18. Straight apostrophes stay straight: the Web build disables Sphinx smart quotes. Runs of spaces in the text layer collapse in HTML.
19. On phones, the overview panels, the LCD map and the Smart Transfer Switch package panel pan sideways inside the figure; the LCD, key-combination and troubleshooting tables scroll inside their containers.
