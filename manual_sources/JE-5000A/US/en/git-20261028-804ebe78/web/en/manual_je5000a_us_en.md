#### SAVE THESE INSTRUCTIONS

<ul><li><p>Make sure to review your local battery recycling guidelines and find a certified recycling center or collection point near you for defective battery disposal. Discontinue use and contact customer support for assistance.</p></li><li><p>Do not charge the battery in extremely hot or cold environments and strictly adhere to the product's specified operating temperature ranges：</p><p>-Charging temperature: 0°C to 45°C (32°F to 113°F);</p><p>-Discharging temperature: -15°C to 45°C (5°F to 113°F);</p><p>-Storage temperature: 0°C to 25°C (32°F to 77°F).</p></li><li><p>To ensure proper air circulation, keep the product vents uncovered. The area where the product is used must have adequate airflow in a cool, dry environment to prevent overheating.</p><p>-Charging in damp or poorly ventilated spaces may cause safety hazards.</p><p>-Water can cause short circuits or damage to the charger, leading to safety risks.</p></li><li><p>Unplug the power cord from a power outlet during a storm.</p></li><li><p>Immediately turn off the product by pressing the power button if it has fallen, been dropped, or exposed to vibrations.</p></li><li><p>Ensure the device(s) are powered off before connecting it to the product.</p></li><li><p>Do not charge the product using a damaged or broken charging cord or plug.</p></li><li><p>Do not use the product to charge any device with a damaged or broken cable or plug.</p></li><li><p>Always unplug the charging cord by pulling the plug, not the cord, to reduce the risk of damage.</p></li><li><p>Ensure the product is properly secured when transporting it in a moving vehicle.</p></li><li><p>DO NOT place the unit upside down or on its side during use or storage.</p></li><li><p>DO NOT place the product on the floor or at a height less than 18 inches (457 mm) above the floor during operation in a workshop or repair facility.</p></li><li><p>DO NOT use the product's accessories with other devices or equipment.</p></li><li><p>Solar charge times depend on weather conditions, place your solar panel where it will get as much direct sunlight as possible.</p></li></ul>

### USER MAINTENANCE INSTRUCTIONS

<p>It is recommended to fully charge the device before using it for the first time. If stored for 3 to 6 months without a charge, its performance may deteriorate, shortening its lifespan. To maintain optimal performance, ensure that the device undergoes a full charge and discharge cycle at least once every 6 months to 1 year.</p>

## UNINTERRUPTIBLE POWER SUPPLY (UPS)

<p>An uninterruptible power supply (UPS) is a type of continual power system that provides automated backup electric power to a load when the mains grid power fails.</p>

<p>The Jackery 5000 Plus is equipped with two UPS modes: Backup UPS and Online UPS.</p>

### BACKUP UPS

<p>The backup UPS function is enabled by default. In the event of a sudden loss of grid power, the Explorer 5000 Plus will automatically switch to stored power within 20 ms to keep your appliances running.</p>

<p>In Backup UPS mode, the unit's peak output reaches 1440W before power outages. As simultaneous charging/discharging is enabled in Bypass Mode, the actual output power is lower than the rated output power in this mode but returns to rated output power during outages.</p>

<figure class="hb-reference-figure" data-component-id="HB-SPECIAL-REFERENCE-FIGURE" data-reference-id="ups" data-source-fragment-sha256="5333a58d37b781d9e6c2ea31f3dd41f3c8573f3ad6c90c0dd04efadad477b034" data-step-captions="embedded"><div class="hb-reference-semantic" data-reference-id="ups.semantic"><img alt="BACKUP UPS" class="hb-reference-art hb-composite-art" src="assets/ups.png"/></div></figure>

<div class="hb-text-panel"><p>Connect the product to a wall outlet with the AC charging cable, then press the AC output button and power your appliances at the same time .</p></div>

### ONLINE UPS

<p>The online UPS mode, which enables 0 ms switching, can be activated in two ways:</p>

<p>1. Enable Online UPS in the Jackery App.</p>

<p>2. Press the DC and AC Power Buttons simultaneously on the Explorer 5000 Plus.</p>

<table class="manual-callout-table manual-callout-table"><tbody><tr><td class="manual-callout-label">Note</td><td class="manual-callout-body"><p>1. The system settings will revert to Backup UPS mode when the product is in Online mode and is restarted. In Backup UPS mode, the output power is limited by the bypass power, with a maximum output power of 1440W.</p><p>2. In UPS mode, the NEMA L14-30R and NEMA 14-50 outlets each provide a 120V output when the product is connected to a wall outlet using the AC charging cable.</p><p>3. In Online UPS mode, it supports 0 ms switching with a maximum output power of 3600W.</p></td></tr></tbody></table>
