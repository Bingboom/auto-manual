### USB OUTPUT ON/OFF

<p>Once the product is turned on, the USB output is enabled by default, allowing you to charge your devices.</p>

<figure class="hb-reference-figure" data-component-id="HB-SPECIAL-REFERENCE-FIGURE" data-reference-id="usb-output" data-source-fragment-sha256="64d57ccd3374dc3b588ac00561d4b3d6e5ec5b59e65fc2af5f9d81f7fa1c041c" data-step-captions="embedded"><div class="hb-reference-semantic" data-reference-id="usb-output.semantic"><img alt="USB OUTPUT ON/OFF: on / Press once / off / Press and hold for 3s" class="hb-reference-art hb-composite-art" src="assets/usb-output.png"/></div></figure>

<p>USB-C1 and USB-C2 are USB-PD Power Source 3 (PS3) high-power output ports. If the connected user device or accessory does not meet safety requirements, there may be a fire risk. Before using these ports, ensure that the connected device or accessory has fire safety protection.</p>

<p>Only connect the Jackery Explorer 240D to devices or accessories that comply with clauses 6.3, 6.4, and 6.5 of IEC/EN/UL 62368-1 (or other equivalent standards).</p>

<p>To obtain maximum output power, use the official Jackery USB-C to USB-C 5A cable (20V DC/5A, 100W; 28V DC/5A, 140W).</p>

#### Energy Saving Mode

<p>To prevent unnecessary battery consumption from forgetting to turn off the output, the product enables Energy Saving Mode by default. If no device is connected, or if the combined output of the USB-A and USB-C ports is 2W or less, the product will automatically turn off the output after 6 hours.</p>

<p><strong>On/Off:</strong> press the Main Power Button three times in a row.</p>

## CHARGING

<p><strong>Green energy first:</strong> We advocate to use the green energy first. This product supports two modes of charging at the same time: solar charging and USB-C charging. When USB-C charging and solar charging are turned on at the same time, the product will give priority to solar charging and both methods will be used to charge the battery at the maximum permissible power.</p>

<p><strong>Fully charge the product before its first use.</strong></p>

<table class="manual-callout-table manual-callout-table"><tbody><tr><td class="manual-callout-label">NOTE</td><td class="manual-callout-body"><ul><li>The recommended charging temperature for the product ranges from -4°F to 113°F (-20°C to 45°C), and the discharging temperature ranges from -4°F to 113°F (-20°C to 45°C). ## STORAGE

<p>Store the product in a dry clean place with proper ventilation. Storage temperature and humidity:</p>

<ul><li><p>1 month: -4°F to 113°F / -20°C to 45°C (0-60%RH)</p></li><li><p>3 months: 32°F to 113°F / 0°C to 45°C (0-60%RH)</p></li><li><p>12 months: 32°F to 77°F / 0°C to 25°C (0-60%RH)</p></li></ul>

<p>If this product is stored for a long period of time (3 months - 6 months) with the power depleted, it may become unchargeable. To prevent this and maintain battery health, it is recommended to check and recharge the product every three months and perform a full charge and discharge cycle at least once every 6 to 12 months.</p>
