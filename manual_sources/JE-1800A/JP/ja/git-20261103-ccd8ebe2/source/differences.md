# Deliberate Web layout differences from the printed JE-1800A JP manual

Wording is never changed. These are layout decisions only.

1. The cover (p1: identity, opening prose, support address and QR) is not on the Web page; printed page numbers are dropped. The print has no paper TOC or back cover.
2. Chapter titles are shared h2 strips. The print letter-spaces them (「8.安 全 上 の ご 注 意」); the Web keeps the characters without the spacing.
3. The specification table title 【Jackery ポータブル電源 1800】 is the HB-TABLE-SPEC section title; the printed colon after each label is kept.
4. Chapter 2.同梱品 follows the specification table as printed; its four cards are HB-SPECIAL-INBOX, followed by the two ※ notes, the ■ change notice and the recycling box, all in printed order.
5. 各部の名称 is two complete panels (front, right-side circle) because the right-side circle shares its print band with the 4.液晶画面 heading.
6. Each 液晶画面 description block (icon, bold title, text) becomes a row of HB-TABLE-LCD-ICON (unnumbered), in printed order, in two tables with the 省エネモード table and frequency notes between them as printed.
7. The ①–⑲ LCD legend printed in three columns is one HB-TABLE-REFERENCE/lcd-descriptions list in number order.
8. The 絵表示について box prints only the two signal rows; the shared HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-SIGNAL draws its 表示/意味 header, and HB-TABLE-SYMBOL-ICON its 記号/意味 header (JE-1000D/JE-1500D JP V2 component chrome, not source text).
9. Each banded 警告/注意 item becomes one HB-CALLOUT-STRIP with the band label; the item's pictogram is not repeated (the symbol meanings are in the symbol table; the 接地 mark of the last 警告 item has no legend row in the print either).
10. 備考 lines in the App manual become HB-CALLOUT-STRIP/note with label 備考 (the printed 「備考：」 prefix is the label).
11. The 2.1 「+」 add-device glyph is native text in HB-SPECIAL-APP/inline-control.
12. The App notice printed after the 免責事項 list stays there as two paragraphs and the complete badge-pair panel.
13. Figure captions and leader labels printed inside panels stay in the panel art and are carried as alt text and IR metadata, not repeated as visible text.
14. On phones, the front overview and LCD map pan sideways inside the figure; the 省エネモード table and recycling box scroll inside the shared table wrapper.
